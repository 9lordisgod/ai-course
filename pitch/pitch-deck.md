## 2. The moment

*Why now*

- 9 Sept 2026 — Canada launches the $13M National AI Literacy Initiative with Amii (part of the 'AI for All' strategy)
- Target: up to 1M post-secondary students and 50,000+ K–12 educators
- CanCode Phase 5: $30M (2026–2028) to reach ~1.1M students and 76,000 teachers
- New Brunswick weaves AI literacy across K–12 from Sept 2026; B.C. embeds it in ADST & Career Education

> **Speaker notes:** Policy tailwinds are unprecedented. Funding is flowing to educators, but ready-to-teach, curriculum-mapped content is scarce.

---

## 3. The problem

*Gap in the classroom*

- No Canada-wide mandatory AI course — every province, district and school improvises
- Teachers get policy PDFs, not lesson plans; 'AI literacy' is a required outcome without a curriculum
- Newcomer families (Chinese is Canada's 3rd most spoken language) are left out of English-only resources
- Existing US-centric tools ignore Canadian privacy law, Indigenous data sovereignty and provincial curricula

> **Speaker notes:** Educators want to comply and teach well, but they lack time, translation and Canadian context.

---

## 4. Our solution

*Product*

- 8 curriculum-mapped modules: How AI works · GenAI & cognitive offloading · Bias & deepfakes · Privacy · Academic integrity · Indigenous data sovereignty · Responsible use · Future careers
- Every lesson: plain-English text, ElevenLabs voice-over, classroom activity, practice quiz, mastery points & certificate
- Beyond K–12: four learning tracks (Intro · Practitioner ML · AI Engineer · Academic) — 16 modules, 48 original lessons built on Microsoft, Hugging Face, fast.ai, d2l.ai, Karpathy and Stanford/MIT open courses
- Open Resource Library: 41 curated curricula, books and classes with license and fork/link-only status — no rehosted copyrighted PDFs
- Teacher Hub: traffic-light AI-use policy generator + disclosure templates · Parent portal · Provincial Policy Explorer
- Privacy-first: no student accounts, progress on-device, works with district-approved tools

> **Speaker notes:** Demo a K–12 lesson: press Listen, take the quiz, watch mastery update. Then open the AI Engineer track and the Open Resource Library, and finish with the policy generator.

---

## 5. Why voice-over matters

*Differentiator*

- Early readers (K–3) and English-language learners can access every lesson
- Accessibility: WCAG-aligned audio alternative for all text content
- ElevenLabs voices deliver studio-quality English narration from one cached pipeline
- Server-side proxy keeps API keys safe; audio cached per lesson to keep cost near zero

> **Speaker notes:** Voice is not a gimmick — it doubles the addressable grade range and satisfies accessibility requirements districts must meet.

## 7. Business model

*Revenue*

- Open-core: lessons free & open-source (MIT) to maximise adoption and trust
- School licence: $1,200/school/yr — analytics dashboard, LMS (Google Classroom / Brightspace) integration, custom voices
- District licence: volume pricing + policy customisation + PD workshops
- Grant-funded delivery: CanCode-eligible nonprofits license content for funded programs
- Optional: paid PD micro-credential for educators (co-badged with Essentials for Educators)

> **Speaker notes:** Free tier drives teacher adoption; districts pay for administration, compliance and integration.
